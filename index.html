About the Temple

80 Years of Sacred Legacy

मिथिला दुर्गामंदिर — स्थापित सन् १९४५ में — मिथिला की पवित्र धरती पर माता दुर्गा की आराधना का केंद्र है। यह मंदिर मिथिला जन कल्याण समिति द्वारा संचालित एवं संरक्षित है।

Founded in 1945, this 80-year-old temple stands as a beacon of Mithila culture, devotion, and community. Governed by the Mithila Jan Kalyan Samiti, it has served countless devotees across generations, keeping the traditions of Maithili worship alive.

The temple celebrates the living tradition of Mithila Painting (Madhubani Kala) — adorning its walls with the ancient art of Mithila, and rings with the sacred Maithili hymns of Vidyapati, passed down through generations of devotees.

मैथिली भाषा

Maithili — Language of Mithila

One of India's oldest living languages — the tongue of poets, the scripture of devotion, and the soul of Mithila.

एक प्राचीन भाषा — An Ancient Language

Maithili (मैथिली) is one of the oldest and richest languages of the Indian subcontinent, with a literary tradition spanning over a thousand years. Spoken by more than 50 million people in the Mithila region of Bihar, Jharkhand, and Nepal's Terai, it carries within it the devotional poetry, folk songs, and cultural wisdom of an entire civilization.

In 2003, Maithili was officially recognized as one of the 22 scheduled languages under the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution — a testament to its literary depth and cultural importance. It predates Hindi as a literary language, emerging from Sanskrit's Apabhramsha forms over a millennium ago.

विद्यापति — The Maithili Kokil

The greatest treasure of Maithili literature is the poet Vidyapati Thakur (c. 1352–1448 CE), born in Bisapi village of Madhubani. Revered as the Maithili Kokil (Nightingale of Mithila) and Abhinavjaydeva, his Padavali — devotional songs addressed to Mata Durga, Radha-Krishna, and Shiva-Parvati — are inseparable from Mithila's temple worship. His Durga stotras are chanted at this very temple during Navratri and other festivals.

जय जय भैरवि, असुर-भयाउनि,
दुर्गति-हरिणि, जग-ताराउनि।

— Vidyapati Thakur, Durga Stuti (Maithili, c. 14th century)

लिपि — Script Heritage

Maithili possesses its own ancient script — Tirhuta (Mithilakshar) — one of South Asia's oldest indigenous writing systems, with roots in the Brahmi script. Today, Maithili is predominantly written in Devanagari (मैथिली) for wider accessibility, while scholars and cultural activists are actively reviving the Tirhuta script as a heritage treasure.

सांस्कृतिक धरोहर — Cultural Legacy

The Maithili-speaking people gave the world an extraordinary cultural heritage: Mithila Painting (Madhubani Kala) — a living art tradition now recognized globally; Saurath Sabha — the world's oldest marriage gathering; Sama Chakeva — a unique sibling-bond festival; and a rich repertoire of folk songs, Jitia, Madhushravani, and Kojagara celebrations. This temple stands as a living center of that culture.

समिति

Mithila Jan Kalyan Samiti

मिथिला जन कल्याण समिति की स्थापना मिथिला संस्कृति, भाषा और धर्म के संरक्षण एवं प्रसार के उद्देश्य से की गई थी।

The Mithila Jan Kalyan Samiti is the governing body of Mithila Durgamandir. It is dedicated to preserving Mithila language, culture, art, and heritage while serving the community through religious and social welfare programs.
